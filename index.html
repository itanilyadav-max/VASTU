🖱️ Canvas Interactions

  • Hover over a corner ● → drag to reshape the floor plan
  • Hover over an edge ◆ → drag to push/pull the wall
  • Click inside a coloured zone → inspect its Vaastu details in the right panel
  • Hover over the gold rotation ring → drag to rotate Vaastu layers
  • Drag on the compass bar at the bottom → set Vaastu rotation
  • Alt + Drag on an uploaded image → reposition it

📐 Right Panel Tabs

  • Zone — detailed info for the selected Vaastu zone
  • 📐 Image — upload a floor plan photo and overlay it on the canvas
  • ✦ AI — run Gemini‑powered Vaastu analysis on your plan
  • 💬 Ask — chat with AI about Vaastu (real web search + sources)

💡 Pro Tips

  • Use the Shape buttons (left panel) to switch between L, U, T, H, C, Z, +, and Courtyard layouts
  • Toggle Vaastu Layers to see zones, pada grid, devta names, attributes, room recommendations, and 16 wall points
  • Brahmasthan (centre) is always shown — the spiritual heart of the plan
  • Upload a photo and use ✦ Analyse to get AI feedback on your actual floor plan
